01 The room

Serious ideas.
Generous room.

Formal methods should feel exacting. They should not feel lonely.

Some Sundays begin with a talk. Others begin with someone opening a laptop and saying, “I’m stuck.” We learn the Lean theorem prover, share work in progress, and help one another move difficult ideas forward.

Bring a theorem, a project, or just questions. New to Lean? You belong here too.

I

Rigor

We care about what holds up—definitions, arguments, and the code beneath them.

II

Craft

We make, test, explain, and improve things together, one proof state at a time.

III

Company

Curiosity is enough to enter. Generosity is how the whole room gets better.
